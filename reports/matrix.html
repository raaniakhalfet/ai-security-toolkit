Reading guide. The firewall filters messages and replies; the guard limits what the agent's tools may do. Each one alone leaves a gap, and together they cover each other. 95% interval = the range the true rate probably lies in (Wilson score). With few runs it is wide: 0 out of 36 only shows the true rate is probably below about 10%, not that it is zero. If two intervals overlap, the difference may be chance. Results come from a small local model, so use them as a rough guide.
